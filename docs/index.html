MoonCam

Your Android phone as a real webcam and microphone for your computer. Over a USB cable or your local Wi-Fi. Local only: no cloud, no account, no telemetry.

Download for Ubuntu 26.04

What it does

The picture appears as a camera called "MoonCam" in OBS Studio, Discord, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and your browser.
Front, back, ultra-wide and telephoto lenses when the phone has them. Resolution, frame rate and quality presets from 480p to 1080p.
Wi-Fi pairing with a six-digit code shown on both screens; TLS 1.3 and paired phones only.
The phone's microphone too, over USB or Wi-Fi, with noise reduction on your computer. It appears as "MoonCam Microphone" in your apps.

Install

From the terminal, download the release and install it:

curl -L -o mooncam.deb https://github.com/Moon-TechnologiesCO/MoonCam/releases/download/v0.1.1/mooncam_0.1.1_amd64.deb
sudo apt install ./mooncam.deb

The package installs everything MoonCam needs: the .NET runtime, FFmpeg, adb and the signed virtual camera driver. On the phone, enable USB debugging and press Connect via USB.

Privacy and cookies

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. The MoonCam app sends your camera's video and microphone audio only to your own computer. Read the full notice in PRIVACY.md.
