MoonCam

Your Android phone as a webcam and a noise-cancelled microphone for Linux. Over a USB cable or your Wi-Fi, with nothing leaving your home.

v0.4 New: the phone's microphone, with noise reduction
Download MoonCam View on GitHub
Free and open source · Ubuntu 26.04 and Android 10 or newer

Everything a good webcam does

Every app on your computer sees MoonCam as ordinary hardware, so there is nothing to set up inside OBS, Zoom or Meet.

A real webcam

A camera called "MoonCam" in OBS Studio, Discord, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and your browser. The driver is signed by Canonical: it works with Secure Boot and needs no reboot.

The phone's microphone

"MoonCam Microphone" appears in your sound settings. Your computer removes hum and background noise before any app hears it.

USB or Wi-Fi

Plug in a cable, or pair once with a six-digit code shown on both screens. After that the phone connects by itself on networks you trust.

Every lens

Front, back, ultra-wide and telephoto when your phone has them. Choose 480p to 1080p, 30 or 60 frames per second and the quality.

Easy on the battery

The phone's camera rests while nobody is watching and wakes in about a second when an app opens it.

Keeps going

If the cable wiggles or the Wi-Fi drops, MoonCam reconnects on its own and skips late frames instead of building up delay.

Install

You need both parts: the app on your phone and MoonCam on your computer.

On your phone

Scan the code with your phone's camera, or open this page on the phone and tap the button.

Download for Android

Android 10 or newer. The app is not in Google Play yet, so Android asks you to allow installing it from your browser.

On your computer

Paste this in a terminal. It downloads the latest release and installs everything MoonCam needs: .NET, FFmpeg, adb and the virtual camera driver.

$ curl -fLO https://github.com/jdswe/MoonCam/releases/latest/download/mooncam-linux-amd64.deb
$ sudo apt install ./mooncam-linux-amd64.deb

Ubuntu 26.04 (64-bit). Prefer clicking? Download the .deb and open it with App Center.

Connect for the first time

  1. Open MoonCam on the computer and the MoonCam app on the phone.
  2. By cable: turn on USB debugging in the phone's developer options, plug it in and press Connect via USB. By Wi-Fi: press Pair and check that the six-digit code matches on both screens.
  3. Pick MoonCam as the camera and MoonCam Microphone as the microphone in Zoom, Meet, Discord or OBS.

Take a look

Private by design

MoonCam has no servers. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to send.

Stays at home

Video and sound go only from your phone to your computer, over the cable or your local network.

Encrypted Wi-Fi

TLS 1.3 with keys pinned at pairing. Only phones you paired can connect.

Asks first

The phone asks before it streams over USB, and its notification shows whenever the camera or microphone is on.

No tracking

No account, no telemetry, no crash reports. This site has no cookies. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Which apps can use MoonCam?

Any app that can use a webcam or a microphone on Linux: OBS Studio, Discord, Zoom, Google Meet and Teams in the browser, Firefox, Chrome and more. Choose "MoonCam" and "MoonCam Microphone" in the app's settings.

Do I need the internet?

Only to download MoonCam. After that, the phone and the computer talk directly over the cable or your own Wi-Fi.

Why does Android warn me when I install the app?

The app is downloaded from this project's GitHub releases instead of Google Play, so Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. The source code is public.

The camera does not appear in my app

Open MoonCam: the setup panel shows what is missing and installs it with one click. Then reopen the app that should use the camera.

How do I turn noise cancellation on or off?

In MoonCam on the computer, use the Noise cancellation switch under Microphone. It takes effect at once, even during a call, and apps keep using "MoonCam Microphone".

How do I uninstall it?

On the computer run sudo apt remove mooncam. On the phone, uninstall it like any other app.
