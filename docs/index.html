Your phone is the best camera you own. Use it on Linux.

MoonCam turns an Android phone into a webcam and a noise-cancelled microphone for your computer, over a USB cable or your Wi-Fi. Everything stays between your phone and your PC.

Download MoonCam View on GitHub

Download

You need both parts: the app on your Android phone (Android 10 or newer) and MoonCam on your computer (Ubuntu 26.04).

On your phone

Scan the code with your phone's camera, or open this page on the phone and tap the button.

Download for Android

The app is not in Google Play yet. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then open the downloaded file.

On your computer

Paste this in a terminal. It downloads the latest release and installs everything MoonCam needs: the .NET runtime, FFmpeg, adb and the signed virtual camera driver.

$ curl -fLO https://github.com/jdswe/MoonCam/releases/latest/download/mooncam-linux-amd64.deb
$ sudo apt install ./mooncam-linux-amd64.deb

Prefer clicking? Download the .deb and open it with App Center.

What it does

Every app on your computer sees MoonCam as ordinary hardware, so there is nothing to set up inside OBS, Zoom or Meet.

A real webcam called "MoonCam"
Works in OBS Studio, Discord, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and your browser. The driver is signed by Canonical, so it works with Secure Boot and needs no reboot.
The phone's microphone, cleaned up
"MoonCam Microphone" appears in your sound settings. Your computer filters out hum and background noise before any app hears it.
USB or Wi-Fi
Plug in a cable, or pair once with a six-digit code shown on both screens. After that the phone connects on its own whenever MoonCam is open on a network you trust.
Every lens on your phone
Front, back, ultra-wide and telephoto when the phone has them. Pick 480p to 1080p, 30 or 60 frames per second, and the quality you want.
Easy on the battery
The phone's camera rests while nobody is watching and wakes in about a second when an app opens the camera.
Keeps going
If the cable wiggles or the Wi-Fi drops, MoonCam reconnects by itself and skips late frames instead of building up delay.

Set up in three steps

The first connection takes about two minutes.

  1. Install both apps

    MoonCam on the computer with the command above, and the app on the phone with the QR code.

  2. Connect

    For USB, turn on USB debugging in the phone's developer options and plug in the cable. For Wi-Fi, press Pair on the computer and confirm the code on the phone.

  3. Pick MoonCam in your app

    Choose "MoonCam" as the camera and "MoonCam Microphone" as the microphone in Zoom, Meet, Discord or OBS.

Private by design

MoonCam has no servers. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to send.

  • Video and sound go only from your phone to your computer, over the cable or your local network.
  • Wi-Fi connections use TLS 1.3, and only phones you paired can connect.
  • No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports.
  • This page uses no cookies and loads nothing from other sites. Read the privacy notice.
