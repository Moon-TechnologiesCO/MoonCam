Cookie policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: this website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. That is why there is no cookie banner.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser to remember you, measure visits or show advertising. Similar techniques include local storage, pixels and fingerprinting.

What this site uses

  • No cookies of any kind: no session, preference, analytics or advertising cookies.
  • No local storage, session storage or other browser storage.
  • No third-party content: fonts, images and code are served from this site itself, so no other company learns that you visited.
  • The only script copies the install command to your clipboard when you press Copy.

The hosting provider

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Pages under github.io do not set cookies for visitors. If you are signed in to GitHub, links to github.com (for example the source code or releases) take you to GitHub, which uses its own cookies under the GitHub cookie policy.

The MoonCam apps

The desktop and phone apps are not websites and use no cookies or trackers.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so before any cookie is used, and a consent choice will be added where the law requires it. Questions: github.com/jdswe/MoonCam/issues.
