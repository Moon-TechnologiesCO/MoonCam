Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: MoonCam does not collect, store or send personal data. Your camera and microphone go only from your phone to your own computer. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no forms.

Who is responsible

MoonCam and this website are made by Jose David Carranza Angarita, an individual developer in Colombia (the "data controller" under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation). Contact: open an issue at github.com/jdswe/MoonCam/issues, or for anything private, a private report on GitHub.

The MoonCam apps

  • Camera and microphone. The phone app uses the camera and the microphone only while it is connected to your computer and streaming. Its notification shows whenever they are on. The video and sound travel only to your computer, over the USB cable or your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Processing on your computer. Your computer decodes the video, removes noise from the sound and hands both to the apps you choose (for example Zoom or OBS). MoonCam does not record or keep them; an app you choose may, under its own policy.
  • Pairing data. Paired phones, their public keys and the Wi-Fi networks you trust are saved only on your computer, in your user folder, readable only by you.
  • Diagnostic log. MoonCam writes a log on your computer (connection events, device model, errors) to help you fix problems. It never leaves your computer unless you share it.
  • No telemetry. No usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or analytics of any kind. MoonCam has no servers.
  • Permissions. The phone app asks for the camera, the microphone, notifications and, on Android 17 and newer, access to the local network. Each is used only for the purpose shown.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages with no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no forms and no third-party scripts or fonts. See the cookie policy.
  • It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page and may keep them in its logs for security. That processing is GitHub's, described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The project has no access to those logs.
  • Downloads are served by GitHub Releases under the same terms.

Legal basis and purpose

Because MoonCam collects nothing, there is no processing by the project that needs a legal basis. The local processing on your devices happens at your request, to provide the service you started (GDPR article 6(1)(b); authorization under article 9 of Law 1581 of 2012 given by starting the stream).

Sharing and international transfers

The project does not share, sell or transfer personal data, because it holds none. Web hosting logs are handled by GitHub, Inc. in the United States under its own policy.

Retention

Nothing is kept by the project. Data on your devices stays until you delete it: uninstalling the package and removing the ~/.config/mooncam and ~/.local/state/mooncam folders erases everything MoonCam saved on your computer; uninstalling the phone app erases its data on the phone.

Your rights

Under Law 1581 of 2012 (habeas data) and, where it applies, the GDPR, you have the right to know, update, correct and delete your personal data, to revoke your authorization and to complain to the authority — in Colombia, the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio. Since the project holds no data about you there is usually nothing to hand over, but you can always ask through the contacts above and you will get an answer within the time limits of Law 1581: 10 business days for questions and 15 for claims.

Children

MoonCam is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date, and the history stays visible in the public repository.
