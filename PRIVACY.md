# Privacy and cookies — MoonCam

**Short version:** MoonCam works between your phone and your computer. Nothing is sent to a cloud, there is no
account, no telemetry and no analytics. The MoonCam website uses no cookies.

## What MoonCam does with your data

- **Camera and microphone:** the video and the microphone audio from your phone go only to your computer, over the
  USB cable or your local Wi-Fi network. The phone records its microphone only while MoonCam is connected and
  streaming; the computer noise-reduces it locally before any app can use it. MoonCam does not store the audio
  unless an app you choose records it.
- **Connection:** over Wi-Fi, the phone and the computer use TLS 1.3. You pair them once with a six-digit code shown on
  both screens, and MoonCam connects only to phones you paired.
- **Pairing data:** paired phones and trusted Wi-Fi networks are stored on your computer only.
- **Logs:** MoonCam writes a diagnostic log on your computer to help you fix problems. It stays there unless you
  share it.
- **No telemetry:** MoonCam does not send crash reports, usage statistics or identifiers anywhere.
- **Network access:** the only downloads are the components you install (adb, FFmpeg, the virtual camera driver),
  installed through Ubuntu's package system.

## Website (MoonCam web page)

- No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts. The page loads only its own files and images.
- Your browser may keep the usual request logs that any web server keeps; the project does not read them.

## Your rights

MoonCam keeps no data about you on any server, so there is nothing to request. To delete everything, uninstall the
package and remove the MoonCam settings folder on your computer.
